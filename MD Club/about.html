About us

Mobile App Development Club

New Horizon College of Engineering · established 2023.

Who we are

A student group that promotes learning and building modern mobile apps. Members work in small teams on practical projects through workshops, hackathons and design challenges each semester.

Our objective

Strengthen mobile programming skills, give students a way to see their ideas become real, and build a community around creativity, teamwork, entrepreneurship and learning.

Outcomes

Better programming and creative skills, familiarity with current tools, and teamwork skills for professional settings.

What we do

Flagship events

Coding challenges, UI design sprints and pitch competitions (Mindspark, InnoWave, TechSync), open to all students.

Hands-on workshops

The full app process, from idea and design to a working build.

Four committees

Program, Project, Marketing and Review keep everything running.
